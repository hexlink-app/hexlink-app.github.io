HEXLink Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

HEXLink lets contributors record everyday interactions, submit videos for review, and track their work and rewards. The individual developer identified as the seller in the App Store operates HEXLink. In this policy, “we” refers to that developer.

1. Information we receive

When you register, we receive your name or username, email address and authentication information. We keep the information needed to verify your email, manage your account and help you recover access. If you enter a Singapore mobile number for PayNow, we record the number and your confirmation that we may use it for payouts. We also keep reward and payment records.

When you confirm an upload in this iOS version, we receive the processed, silent video described in section 2, your submission notes, and information linking the submission to your account and task. We keep file details such as name, type, size, duration and dimensions, together with upload status, review decisions and timestamps. Our hosting provider may process connection information and service logs to deliver and secure the service.

2. Recording, face replacement and your choice to upload

When you record a video or choose one from your device, HEXLink uses a local copy as a draft. You can manage camera, microphone and photo access in your device settings. If the source video includes audio, it remains present in the source copy until that copy is removed.

HEXLink uses videos to help robots learn from human actions and interactions. We analyse or annotate facial movements, expressions and inferred emotions to help robotics models learn from interactions. Emotion labels are interpretations of visible behaviour, not verified facts about a person’s internal emotional state. Identifying the people shown is not the purpose of this work.

Before upload, the app replaces detected faces with artificial faces on your device, with the aim of preserving useful expressions and movements while removing the original facial appearance. It also removes audio. You can preview the processed video and decide whether to upload it.

To perform face replacement, the app temporarily uses face images and the positions of facial features, such as the eyes and mouth. These working images and positions are not saved separately or sent to HEXLink or an external face-processing service. The original recording and its audio are not uploaded through this flow.

Face replacement may be incomplete. The app prompts you to check the processed video, including sections with reported processing issues. Faces or other details, such as names or reflections, may still identify someone. You can retry processing, edit the video or choose not to upload it. Only record and share material you are authorized to contribute. Uploaded videos undergo further privacy processing and review before the uses described in section 3.

3. How we use information

We use account and submission information to provide tasks, review contributions, communicate decisions and revision requests, and administer rewards and payouts. We also use information to operate, troubleshoot and protect the service, respond to requests and meet applicable obligations.

Approved video materials have two possible uses:

  • HEXLink uses them internally to train models for humanoid robots, including models for handling objects and moving through their environment.
  • HEXLink prepares edited videos and annotations for sale or licence to clients, such as robot manufacturers, for their own robotics model training.

The materials may include annotations of facial expressions and inferred emotions. We do not use these annotations to identify people or build profiles of named individuals.

Before either use, we apply further privacy processing and review to remove information that could reasonably identify a person. Materials that do not meet this requirement are excluded from training and delivery. Personal identity is neither necessary for our robotics work nor a permitted input to these uses. This restriction applies equally to our internal training and customer deliverables. Account, contact and payout information is used separately to administer the service, as described above.

The current app does not display third-party advertisements or use a third-party advertising SDK.

4. Storage, access and sharing

The app processes the source video on your device. When you confirm an upload, the processed video goes to HEXLink’s private storage, currently hosted by Supabase. Only authorized HEXLink personnel can view videos awaiting review, including any faces or other identifying details that remain in the processed video. Approved videos remain in our private storage. Supabase also provides authentication and database services. We may change storage providers and will update this policy to identify the provider we use.

After approval, HEXLink further edits the silent, processed footage before supplying edited clips and annotations to robot manufacturers. Manufacturers may use the delivered materials to train their own robotics models. Under our Submission Terms, we may supply these materials only if no person can reasonably be identified from them, either on their own or together with other information reasonably available to the manufacturer. This condition applies to the whole clip and its annotations, including details such as a visible name badge. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

On your device. The app temporarily uses face images and the positions of facial features, such as the eyes and mouth, to replace faces. It does not save these working images or positions separately. It keeps the draft video, processed video segments and processing progress locally so that you can resume and review your work. You can discard a draft. After a successful submission the app runs a cleanup step to remove its draft copy and processing files. Removing the app also removes its local app data. If you selected a video from your photo library, the library copy remains under your control.

Uploaded videos. We delete rejected and abandoned uploads. We retain approved videos while preparing and using them for the purposes in section 3. We delete video copies held by HEXLink within six months after all applicable internal training and client delivery uses of that video have been completed. If a video has both uses, the six-month period begins after both are complete; if it has only one use, it begins after that use is complete. This period does not begin on upload. The same schedule applies to associated facial-expression and inferred-emotion annotations held by HEXLink, and to any identifying details remaining in the stored video. You can request deletion of an individual video by emailing the address in section 7. We will remove that video from storage under HEXLink’s control, currently hosted by Supabase.

Products already delivered. The six-month deletion period applies to HEXLink-controlled copies, not materials already delivered to clients. Under our Submission Terms, clients may retain and use delivered materials that no longer reasonably identify anyone under the rights already granted, including after HEXLink deletes its copies or your account. We retain account and payout records as needed to operate the service and meet applicable obligations.

Deletion requests. Go to Profile > Request account deletion in the app. We aim to complete deletion of the account and associated data within 30 days of the request and will confirm completion at your registered email address. Removing the app from your device does not delete an uploaded submission. Contact us to request access or correction, or to withdraw consent where applicable. We may retain limited records required by law or to resolve a payment, dispute or security issue. Withdrawing consent needed for a feature may prevent us from providing that feature.

6. Updates

We will post changes here with a new effective date and provide any additional notice or obtain any permission required before new uses or disclosures begin.

7. Contact
