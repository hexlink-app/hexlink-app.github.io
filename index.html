HEXLink

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026

This policy explains how information is handled when you use the HEXLink mobile app. HEXLink lets contributors learn recording requirements, submit selected photos or videos for review and processing, and view the status of their work and rewards. HEXLink is the name of the app, not a separate legal entity. The app is operated by the individual developer identified as its seller in the App Store; “we” refers to that developer.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you register, we receive your name or username, email address, and authentication information. We also keep your account status and information needed to verify your email, sign you in, and help you recover access.

PayNow information. We receive the Singapore mobile number you enter for PayNow payouts and record your confirmation that we may use it for that purpose. Entering the number does not verify ownership of the number or its PayNow registration. We may keep records of rewards and payments associated with your account.

Submissions. We receive the photos and videos you choose to upload, including any audio in the videos, notes you enter, task and submission identifiers, review decisions, and related timestamps. Media you submit may contain people, voices, places, or other personal information. Please follow the task instructions and avoid including information you are not authorized to share.

Technical information. To prepare, upload, and review media, we process file names, types and sizes; video duration, dimensions, orientation and audio presence; capture source and device platform; and upload status. Our hosting provider may process connection information and service logs needed to deliver and secure the service.

2. Camera, microphone, photos, and drafts

HEXLink requests camera and microphone permission when you choose to record, and photo library permission when you choose media from your device. You can manage these permissions in your device settings. Your recording drafts are stored on your device before you submit them. HEXLink uploads media you select when you choose to submit it. Removing the app may remove local drafts, but does not automatically remove submissions already uploaded.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, and provide account support.
  • To show tasks, receive submissions, review work, and communicate decisions or revision requests.
  • To process submissions and create Processed Products that do not identify any person, and to sell or license those products to business customers. “Processed Products” are results created by editing, transforming, annotating, aggregating, analysing, or otherwise processing submissions. Our Terms describe the licence for this use.
  • To administer contributor rewards and PayNow payouts.
  • To operate, troubleshoot, and protect HEXLink and comply with applicable obligations.

The current HEXLink app does not display third-party advertisements or use a third-party advertising SDK.

4. Who can access information

HEXLink receives and reviews submissions. Authorized reviewers and people operating the service may access account details and submissions as needed for their work. We use Supabase to provide authentication, database services, and private media storage.

We may provide Processed Products to business customers for their independent use, but only where the products delivered to them do not identify any person or make any person reasonably identifiable, alone or together with other information reasonably available to the customer. We do not provide original submissions or identifiable media to business customers for their independent use. If we introduce additional uses or recipients of personal information, we will update this policy and obtain any permission required before that use or disclosure begins. This policy does not grant permission to use personal information in a video for an unspecified purpose.

We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service and its users. Uploaded submissions are not displayed in a public social feed.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Drafts remain on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app. We keep uploaded submissions while needed for review, creation of Processed Products, task fulfillment, and any related dispute; account and payout records are kept while needed to operate the service and meet applicable obligations. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed for those purposes. Processed Products that no longer identify any person may continue to be used or licensed after the original submission or account is deleted.

You can manage camera, microphone, and photo permissions in your device settings and choose whether to submit media. In the app, go to Profile > Request account deletion to submit a deletion request. We aim to complete account and associated-data deletion within 30 days and will confirm completion at your registered email address. To request access or correction of your information, or to withdraw consent where applicable, contact us below. We may need to retain limited records required by law or to resolve a payment, dispute, or security issue. Withdrawing consent needed for a feature may prevent us from providing that feature.

6. Updates

We may update this policy when our practices change. We will post the revised policy here with a new effective date and provide any additional notice required by law.
